LavaLoon trades speed for safety. The Hounds are not there to deal damage —
they are there to be the thing Air Defences shoot at while your Balloons work
through the base. Every decision is about keeping that relationship intact.

## The shape of the attack

1. **Clear the outside.** A few Balloons or a Warden-less side push to strip
   the outer buildings, so the main force does not path around the base.
2. **Hounds first, wide.** Spread them so they split Air Defence aggro rather
   than stacking on one.
3. **Balloons behind the Hounds.** Never level with them, never ahead. Balloons
   in front of a Hound are Balloons in front of an Air Defence.
4. **Warden walks with the Balloons.** The aura matters more than his damage.
   Keep him behind the front rank and use the Eternal Tome for the first big
   splash hit.

## Spell timing

This is where LavaLoon attacks are won or lost.

- **Rage on clusters, not on single defences.** A rage that covers four
  Balloons hitting one Wizard Tower is wasted.
- **Haste through open ground.** Balloons are slow; the time they spend
  travelling is time Air Defences spend shooting.
- **Freeze reactively.** Hold at least one for Air Sweepers and one for the
  moment a Hound pops and the Balloons behind it get exposed.

## Common failure

The usual loss looks like this: the Hounds die earlier than expected, the
Balloons continue as a clump, and a single Air Defence deletes the group. If
you see a Hound about to pop, freeze rather than rage — surviving the transition
matters more than the extra damage.
