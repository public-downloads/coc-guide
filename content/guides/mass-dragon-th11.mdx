Mass Dragon is the attack you learn on. It asks very little of your micro and
almost everything of your funnel. If the Dragons walk through the middle, you
three-star; if they ring around the outside, you two-star and wonder why.

## Build the funnel first

Pick the side with the most Air Defences reachable in a straight line. Then
spend real resources clearing the two corners either side of it — the Queen
with a couple of Balloons, or a few Dragons of your own. Do not start the main
deployment until both corners are dead.

A funnel is not decoration. Dragons path toward the nearest building; if
buildings remain on the flank, the nearest building is on the flank.

## Deploying

- **Spread Dragons along the funnel edge**, not in one clump — spread limits
  Seeking Air Mine damage.
- **Balloons behind them**, aimed at the Air Defences the Dragons are already
  walking toward.
- **Rage when they hit the first defended compartment**, not on the outskirts.
- **Save one Freeze for the Air Sweeper** if it is pointed into your approach.

## What usually goes wrong

- **The funnel was too shallow.** One corner cleared, not two.
- **Everything dropped at once.** Dragons deployed in a line take splash as a
  line.
- **Rage spent early.** On the way in, rather than on the compartment that
  actually stalls them.

## Moving on from it

Mass Dragon stops working when Inferno Towers and Air Defence levels outpace
Dragon HP. When you find yourself two-starring bases you used to three-star,
that is the signal to learn a hybrid or a Queen charge rather than to keep
tuning this one.
