Open with a Queen / RC hybrid walk. Use the Queen and Healers to destroy a part from the Base and act as funneling for the main army, while the RC uses the invisibility spells to snipe a few stronger buildings (e.g. Scattershots, Monolith, Air Defenses).
Once you did that either recall the RC or Queen depending on who needs it more and place the E-Dragon to funnel from a different side. After that you place your main army
        - the jump can be used as a first step to get over the Walls- 
Afterwards you can use the Earthquakeboots on the King to destroy the rest of the walls and enable the super Bowlers free passage into the core of the Base.

The Monlith Arrow only does damage before you place Troops - this doesn't include troops from Siege Machines and Abilities or other Heroes. This means the healers should be the only Troops you place apart from the Queen, RC and maybe the Siege Machienes


## Reading the base

Before you place anything, find three things:

- **The entry side.** You want a stretch of compartments where the Queen can
  chew inward without immediately touching an Inferno or the Monolith.
- **The Air Defence positions.** Your healers die to Air Defences and Seeking
  Air Mines. The charge path has to keep them out of range for as long as
  possible.

## Executing the charge

1. **Funnel both sides.** Two small groups on the corners next to your entry
   point, so the Queen's pathing points inward instead of running the perimeter.
2. **Drop the Queen, then healers behind her.** Four to five healers, dropped
   behind her, not in front — they should never enter defence range first.
3. **Hold the Invisibility or ability for the Inferno.** Whatever your saving
   throw is, it exists for a single-target Inferno or Monolith.

The charge is going well if she is opening compartments faster than her healers
are taking damage. If the healers start dying, you are behind — commit the
remaining spells rather than saving them.
